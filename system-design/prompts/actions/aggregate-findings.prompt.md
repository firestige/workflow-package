# SD-10 Aggregate and Triage Findings

After the review barrier closes, read all structured Review results. Preserve provenance; merge only clearly corroborating Findings; relate shared causes; keep materially different impacts separate; flag contradictions without voting.

Route each item to evidence research, Brief change, Skeleton redesign, Draft revision, Reviewer clarification, or Human Decision admission. Reject malformed Findings back to their Reviewer. Do not edit the Design, alter technical conclusions, or close Findings.
